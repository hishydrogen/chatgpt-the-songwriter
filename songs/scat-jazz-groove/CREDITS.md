# Checkpoint 2 groove credits

Original composition, written MIDI performance and arrangement: Codex, following
the user's direction of energetic jazz with a floating scat solo and a rich session.
This is a groove audition, not the final release.

Voice: **Milk**, by **Xepheris**, rendered with `songwriter.voice` and WORLD.
Voicebank source: https://github.com/oxygen-dioxide/milk-ja
Original terms: `libs/voice/milk-ja/license.md`.
The terms allow sharing rendered vocals and porting to other singing synthesizers.
Commercial use requires individual approval from the author. No bank audio files
or official character art are distributed with this checkpoint.
