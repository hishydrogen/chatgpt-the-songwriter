# Checkpoint 1 credits

- Composition, arrangement and programming: Codex for hishydrogen.
- Style reference: The Beatles, “Come Together” (1969); user supplied the 2019 mix.
  The reference recording is used only for analysis, never sampled or included in outputs.
- Virtuosity Drums — Versilian Studios / Karoryfer. CC0 1.0.
  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums
- Black and Blue Basses — Karoryfer. CC0 1.0.
  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-blue-basses
- Black and Green Guitars — Karoryfer / Brian Wood, black Hofner Club guitar samples.
  Upstream permits royalty-free commercial and non-commercial use.
  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-green-guitars
- Emily guitar — D. Smolken / Karoryfer, direct recordings of an Epiphone guitar.
  Upstream permits royalty-free commercial and non-commercial use.
  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.emilyguitar
- jRhodes3d — Jeff Learman, sampled 1977 Rhodes Mark I Stage 73.
  This is a later sampled instrument used for its tonal family, not a claim of exact
  period hardware. The upstream LICENSE permits musicians' works under CC0;
  redistribution of the raw samples is CC BY-NC 4.0. Raw samples are not included here.
  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/jlearman.jRhodes3d
- Wurlitzer EP200 — Greg Sullivan, SFZ mappings by kinwie. CC BY 3.0.
  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/GregSullivan.E-Pianos
- Offline sample rendering: sfizz. Guitar amp processing: guitarix.
  Mixing: LSP EQ/compression/limiter, ZamTube, Dragonfly reverb, repository tape DSP.

All library sources and their licensing files remain in the environment's library
folder. Raw sample libraries and the uploaded reference are not part of this commit.
