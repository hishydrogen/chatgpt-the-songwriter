# Checkpoint 1 audition credits

Original composition, MIDI performance and arrangement: Codex, from the user's
direction of energetic jazz with a floating scat lead and a full session ensemble.
This is a sound audition, not the final song or release.

Voices are rendered by `songwriter.voice` / WORLD from these UTAU voicebanks:

- Hitsuboku Kumi Act4 (筆墨クミ), Cubialpha —
  https://github.com/oxygen-dioxide/hitsuboku-kumi-ja-act4
- Hikari One Crystal, Kyomiii —
  https://github.com/oxygen-dioxide/hikari-one-crystal-ja
- Milk, Xepheris — https://github.com/oxygen-dioxide/milk-ja

Each bank's original terms are kept in its directory under `libs/voice/`.
Kumi's terms permit commercial use with attribution. Hikari and Milk require
contacting their authors before commercial release; these candidates are auditions.
No official character artwork is used.

Sampled instruments:

- Salamander Grand Piano, Yamaha C5 — sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
- Splendid Grand Piano, Steinway-derived — sfzinstruments/SplendidGrandPiano
- jRhodes3d, 1977 Rhodes Mark I — sfzinstruments/jlearman.jRhodes3d
- Double bass — sfzinstruments/dsmolken.double-bass
- Black and Blue Basses — sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-blue-basses
- Black and Green Guitars — sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-green-guitars
- Virtuosity Drums — sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums
- DrumGizmo DRSKit — sfzinstruments/DrumGizmo.DRSKit
- Trombone, French horn, Harmon-muted trumpet — VSCO 2 Community Edition,
  sgossner/VSCO-2-CE; locally generated SFZ mappings
- MTG Solo Saxophones — sfzinstruments/MTG.SoloSax

Library versions and upstream commits are recorded in each `.fetched` file in
`libs/`; original license and attribution files remain with the libraries.
Rendering: sfizz. Processing: LSP EQ/compressor/limiter, Dragonfly Room/Plate Reverb.
No reference recording or third-party melody was sampled.
